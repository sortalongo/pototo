## Summary / Direction

This post reveals Cambra as the solution to the fragmentation problem described in the previous post.

**The metallurgy metaphor:**
- Software systems today are like unworked metal: rigid crystalline structures with grain boundaries (the seams between services, databases, APIs)
- Stress concentrates at these boundaries—that's where bugs happen, where changes break things, where optimization stops
- The structure looks solid but fractures under pressure

**The transformation:**
- A forge/annealing chamber transforms brittle metal by unifying its internal structure—the grain boundaries dissolve, stress flows through instead of concentrating
- Cambra = a chamber for transformation—where fragmented systems go in and unified, ductile systems come out

**The name:**
- "Cambra" alludes to both "cambiare" (to change/transform) and "cámara" (chamber)
- A chamber for transformation

**Connection to previous post:**
- Previous post planted the metaphor (brittleness, grain boundaries, need for a forge)
- This post: "We're building that chamber. We call it Cambra."
